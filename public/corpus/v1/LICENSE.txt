Lexio vocabulary corpus (public/corpus/v1/*.json)

This word list is original content compiled by the Lexio project team
(word selection, CEFR-style band assignment, and Vietnamese glosses),
generated by scripts/build-corpus.ts from scripts/corpus-source-data.ts.

provenance note and the intended future data source.
